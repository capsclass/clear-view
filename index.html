The idea

More awareness.
More confidence.

Everyday environments can be difficult to navigate without visual information. ClearView explores how wearable technology could make useful context easier to access.

◉

Understand the scene

Offer spoken descriptions of relevant objects and nearby surroundings, helping users build a clearer mental picture.

▤

Access printed information

Explore a hands-free way to read signs, labels, and other short text aloud when supported by the device and software.

♫

Keep information hands-free

Pair concise audio feedback with wearable technology so people can keep their hands available for daily tasks.

The experience

Simple in concept.
Meaningful in practice.

A potential user flow that puts relevant information first and avoids overwhelming the wearer.

STEP 01

Capture context

A compatible wearable device could provide visual input, subject to device capabilities and user consent.

STEP 02

Interpret information

AI software could identify useful scene details or text, with clear limits and careful validation.

STEP 03

Deliver audio

Short, understandable spoken descriptions could help users decide what to do next while staying in control.

Technology should open up the world—not add another barrier.

ClearView’s vision is to make useful visual context more accessible, helping people move through everyday moments with greater confidence and independence.
